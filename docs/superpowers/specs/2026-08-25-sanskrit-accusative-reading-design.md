# Sanskrit Accusative Reading Design

## Goal

Add one source-backed accusative form and the Level 4 sentence it unlocks.

## Fixed scope

Exactly two Sanskrit lessons from Wikner 6.B.2/6.B.4(c)2:

- `skt-word-balam`: `बालम्` (*bālam*), “the girl” (feminine accusative singular), using existing `bā`, `la`, and final `m` lessons.
- `skt-sentence-ashvah-naram-ca-balam-ca-vrksam-vahati`: `अश्वः नरम् च बालम् च वृक्षम् वहति` — “The horse carries the man and the girl to the tree.”

Wikner's PDF p.60 gives the *bālam* declension cell, p.62 gives the Roman-transliterated sentence, and p.135 supplies its printed English. The manifest Devanagari follows already attested components. Inventory: 273 Sanskrit lessons (119 letters, 103 words, 51 sentences); Tamil unchanged.
